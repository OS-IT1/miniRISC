MUL writes the low 32 bits of the signed 32 × 32 product to `rd`.
MAC computes `rd <- rd + rs1 * rs2` with 32-bit wraparound. Both use the
R-format field layout: `opcode[31:27], rs1[26:23], rs2[22:19], rd[18:15]`.
